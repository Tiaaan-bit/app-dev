#  My Favorite Series: *Stranger Things*

![Stranger Things Banner](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/f/f7/Stranger_Things_season_4.jpg)

##  Overview
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction horror series created by the *Duffer Brothers* and released on **Netflix**.  
The story is set in the 1980s in the fictional town of *Hawkins, Indiana*, where a group of kids encounters secret government experiments, supernatural forces, and a strange girl named Eleven.

---

##  Main Characters
- **Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown)** – A girl with telekinetic powers  
- **Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard)** – The brave leader of the group  
- **Dustin Henderson (Gaten Matarazzo)** – The comic genius  
- **Lucas Sinclair (Caleb McLaughlin)** – The realist of the group  
- **Jim Hopper (David Harbour)** – The town’s police chief
